
# Call Outcomes

A practice call does not have to end with a booked meeting to be successful.

## Outcome 1 — 15–20 Minute Overview Scheduled

The contact agrees to an introductory conversation.

Scheduling may happen:

- live on the call, or
- through a Microsoft Bookings link sent by email.

## Outcome 2 — 60-Minute Analyst Briefing Scheduled

The contact is ready for a deeper briefing with the named analyst.

## Outcome 3 — Referral Secured

The caller obtains the correct contact or department.

## Outcome 4 — Email Follow-Up

The contact agrees to receive additional information.

Where appropriate, the email may include a Microsoft Bookings link.

## Outcome 5 — Future Follow-Up

The contact asks to reconnect at a later time.

## Outcome 6 — Hard Decline

The contact clearly declines.

The caller should exit professionally.

## Outcome 7 — Wrong Contact / No Referral

The caller confirms the person is not relevant but cannot secure a referral.

## Success Rule

A booked meeting is not the only successful outcome.

The caller should be evaluated on whether they handled the situation appropriately.
