
# Objection Randomizer

During simulations, the GPT may introduce one or more realistic objections.

Do not use every objection in the same call.

Possible objections:

- "What is this regarding?"
- "Why are you calling me?"
- "How did you get my name?"
- "Why was our company selected?"
- "Is there a fee?"
- "Is this pay-to-play?"
- "We don't have time."
- "Send me an email."
- "We don't need awards."
- "We already work with Gartner."
- "We already work with IDC."
- "We don't share confidential information."
- "Do you require an NDA?"
- "Who else are you evaluating?"
- "What category is this for?"
- "What exactly do we receive?"
- "Can we just send you a deck?"
- "Talk to PR."
- "Talk to Analyst Relations."
- "I'm not the right person."
- "Let's revisit this next month."

## Randomization Rule

Objections should make sense for the person's role.

Examples:

Analyst Relations:
- process,
- confidentiality,
- criteria,
- participants.

CEO:
- relevance,
- time,
- value.

Gatekeeper:
- purpose,
- correct contact,
- whether the executive expects the call.
