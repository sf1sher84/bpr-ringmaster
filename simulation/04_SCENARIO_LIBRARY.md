
# Scenario Library

## Scenario 1 — CEO Unfamiliar with Frost & Sullivan

The contact:
- is senior,
- has limited time,
- does not know Best Practices Recognition,
- wants to know why the company was contacted.

Goal:
Schedule a 15–20 minute overview.

---

## Scenario 2 — VP Strategy

The contact:
- understands market research,
- is curious,
- asks why the company stood out.

Goal:
Schedule a 15–20 minute overview or identify the correct briefing participant.

---

## Scenario 3 — Chief Product Officer

The contact:
- is interested in the analyst's research,
- wants to know what the briefing will cover.

Goal:
Schedule a 60-minute analyst briefing.

---

## Scenario 4 — Analyst Relations Manager

The contact:
- understands analyst processes,
- asks about scope, participants, criteria, and timing.

Goal:
Secure the appropriate next meeting.

---

## Scenario 5 — PR / Communications

The contact:
- wants written information,
- wants to manage executive access.

Goal:
Explain the research process and secure the appropriate next step.

---

## Scenario 6 — Gatekeeper

The contact:
- wants to know what the call is about,
- will not automatically transfer the call.

Goal:
Get connected to or referred to the correct person.

---

## Scenario 7 — "Send Me an Email"

The contact:
- does not want to schedule immediately.

Goal:
Send the email and, where appropriate, include the Microsoft Bookings link.

---

## Scenario 8 — Pay-to-Play Concern

The contact:
- asks whether there is a fee,
- is skeptical of recognition programs.

Goal:
Clearly separate research evaluation from later commercial opportunities.

---

## Scenario 9 — Confidentiality Concern

The contact:
- worries about proprietary information.

Goal:
Reassure without making unsupported promises.

---

## Scenario 10 — Existing Frost & Sullivan Recipient

The contact:
- already understands the program,
- does not need the basic overview.

Goal:
Move toward a 60-minute analyst briefing.

---

## Scenario 11 — Wrong Contact

The contact:
- is not responsible for the relevant area.

Goal:
Obtain a referral.

---

## Scenario 12 — Soft No

The contact:
- has engaged in the conversation,
- says they do not think a meeting is necessary.

Goal:
Clarify the hesitation and make one appropriate second ask.

---

## Scenario 13 — Hard No

The contact:
- clearly declines,
- does not want additional discussion.

Goal:
Respect the answer and exit professionally.
