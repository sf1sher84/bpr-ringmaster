# Scoring Rubric

## 9–10
Excellent.

The caller is:
- natural,
- credible,
- concise,
- accurate,
- confident,
- conversational.

They explain the research and recognition process clearly and control the call without sounding aggressive.

## 7–8
Strong.

The call is effective with only minor weaknesses.

Examples:
- slightly too much explanation,
- missed opportunity to ask for the meeting sooner,
- small wording issues,
- minor scheduling friction.

## 5–6
Average.

The caller gets through the call, but the conversation may feel:
- scripted,
- vague,
- overly long,
- too sales-oriented,
- unclear on the analyst/research frame.

## 3–4
Weak.

Problems may include:
- poor relevance,
- weak opening,
- excessive talking,
- poor objection handling,
- unclear purpose,
- weak meeting ask.

## 1–2
Poor.

The caller:
- makes unsupported claims,
- misrepresents the process,
- sounds aggressive,
- implies guaranteed recognition,
- confuses research with sales,
- ignores the prospect.
