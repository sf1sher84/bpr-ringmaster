# Call Scorecard

Score each area from 1–10.

## Opening
Did the caller sound natural, confident, and concise?

## Research Positioning
Did the caller clearly explain that the outreach is research-led?

## Named Analyst Frame
Did the caller correctly position the named analyst as leading the research?

## Relevance
Did the caller explain why the company is relevant without inventing facts?

## Conversational Tone
Did the caller sound human rather than scripted?

## Best Practices Recognition Explanation
Did the caller explain the program clearly and accurately?

## Third-Party Validation
Did the caller communicate the value of independent validation?

## Listening
Did the caller respond to what the prospect actually said?

## Questioning
Did the caller ask useful, relevant questions without turning the call into a sales discovery call?

## Objection Handling
Did the caller acknowledge, clarify, reassure, and respond appropriately?

## Meeting Framing
Did the caller clearly explain the purpose of the 15–20 minute overview or 60-minute briefing?

## Scheduling
Did the caller use a clear, low-friction scheduling approach?

## Accuracy
Did the caller avoid unsupported claims?

## Call Control
Did the caller keep the conversation moving without sounding pushy?

## Overall Score
Provide an overall score from 1–10.
