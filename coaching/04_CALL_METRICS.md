
# Call Metrics

Use these metrics to evaluate outbound performance over time.

Do not judge performance based on one individual call.

Look for patterns.

## Core Outreach Metrics

### Dial-to-Connect Rate

Percentage of outbound attempts that result in a live conversation.

### Connect-to-Right-Person Rate

Percentage of live conversations where the caller reaches the correct contact.

### Right-Person-to-Conversation Rate

Percentage of correct contacts who engage in a meaningful conversation.

### Conversation-to-15-Minute-Overview Rate

Percentage of meaningful conversations that result in a 15–20 minute overview being scheduled.

### 15-Minute Overview Show Rate

Percentage of scheduled overview meetings that actually occur.

### 15-Minute-to-60-Minute Briefing Conversion Rate

Percentage of overview meetings that progress to a 60-minute analyst briefing.

### 60-Minute Briefing Show Rate

Percentage of scheduled analyst briefings that actually occur.

### Referral Rate

Percentage of wrong-contact conversations that result in a useful referral.

### Email Follow-Up Rate

Percentage of conversations that result in the prospect requesting or accepting follow-up information.

### Microsoft Bookings Conversion Rate

Percentage of contacts who receive a Microsoft Bookings link and successfully schedule a meeting.

### Soft-No Recovery Rate

Percentage of soft-no situations that result in a legitimate next step after one appropriate second ask.

### No-Show Rate

Percentage of scheduled meetings where the prospect does not attend.

## Objection Metrics

Track how often the team hears:

- "Send me an email."
- "I don't have time."
- "Is there a fee?"
- "Is this pay-to-play?"
- "Why us?"
- "Who nominated us?"
- "We don't need awards."
- "We already work with Gartner."
- "We already work with IDC."
- "We don't share confidential information."
- "Do we need an NDA?"
- "Talk to PR."
- "Talk to Analyst Relations."
- "I'm not the right person."
- "Let's revisit this later."

## Call Quality Metrics

Also track:

- average score by caller,
- opening score,
- objection-handling score,
- meeting-framing score,
- scheduling score,
- accuracy score,
- call-control score.

## Coaching Use

Use metrics to identify where improvement is needed.

Examples:

Low right-person rate:
- contact data issue,
- targeting issue.

Low conversation rate:
- opener issue,
- relevance issue.

Low meeting rate:
- value explanation,
- objection handling,
- meeting framing.

Low show rate:
- meeting value,
- follow-up quality,
- calendar clarity,
- timing.

High email-only rate:
- meeting ask may be too weak,
- caller may be giving up too early.

Low Microsoft Bookings conversion:
- booking link may not be sent promptly,
- email copy may need improvement,
- meeting value may not be clear.

## Important Rule

Use metrics as coaching signals, not as proof that one script works for every market, role, or industry.
