
# Meeting Type Matrix

## Purpose

Use this file as a quick-reference decision aid for determining the most appropriate next conversation in Frost & Sullivan Best Practices Recognition outreach.

This file does not redefine the meetings.

For full guidance, use:

- knowledge/06_15_MINUTE_OVERVIEW.md
- knowledge/07_60_MINUTE_BRIEFING.md
- knowledge/16_SCHEDULING.md
- knowledge/25_PREVIOUS_RECIPIENT_PATH.md

If this matrix appears to conflict with those Knowledge files, the Knowledge files take precedence.

## Core Rule

Do not ask for more time than the situation warrants.

The typical progression is:

Initial Outreach
→ 15-to-20-Minute Overview
→ 60-Minute Analyst Briefing Where Appropriate

Some previous participants or highly familiar contacts may move directly to the analyst briefing.

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Situation | Typical Next Step |
|---|---|
| Contact is unfamiliar with Frost & Sullivan | 15-to-20-minute overview |
| Contact knows Frost & Sullivan but not Best Practices Recognition | 15-to-20-minute overview |
| Contact requested basic program information | 15-to-20-minute overview |
| Contact is interested but cautious | 15-to-20-minute overview |
| Contact asked for email first | Email follow-up plus 15-to-20-minute overview option |
| Contact already completed the overview | Consider 60-minute analyst briefing |
| Contact is highly familiar with BPR and ready for substantive discussion | Consider 60-minute analyst briefing |
| Previous participant but new contact | Establish familiarity first |
| Previous participant and personally familiar | Overview or analyst briefing depending on context |
| Previous recognition recipient and ready for deeper discussion | Often 60-minute analyst briefing |
| Wrong contact | Referral |
| Analyst Relations controls the process | Coordinate through AR |
| PR or Communications controls the process | Coordinate through their process |
| Busy but interested | Email plus Microsoft Bookings |
| Timing is the main issue | Future follow-up |
| Clear hard no | Close professionally |

## 15-to-20-Minute Overview

Use when the contact needs:

- Frost & Sullivan context
- Best Practices Recognition context
- explanation of why the company surfaced
- explanation of nomination for evaluation
- process overview
- questions answered
- clarification before committing to a deeper conversation

Preferred duration:

15 to 20 minutes

Do not describe the overview as a deep dive.

## 60-Minute Analyst Briefing

Use when the contact:

- already understands Frost & Sullivan
- already understands Best Practices Recognition
- has completed the introductory overview
- has participated previously
- is highly engaged
- is ready for a deeper analyst discussion

Possible discussion areas:

- strategy
- innovation
- growth
- customer value
- differentiation
- market leadership
- recent developments
- relevant evaluation areas

Preferred duration:

60 minutes

## Referral Path

Use when the current contact:

- is not the right person
- lacks visibility into the relevant areas
- refers the caller elsewhere
- needs another team involved

A referral is a valid successful outcome.

## Future Follow-Up Path

Use when:

- timing is the primary issue
- the prospect asks to reconnect later
- the company cannot engage immediately

Confirm:

- follow-up timing
- whether information should be sent
- whether the Microsoft Bookings link should be included

## Guardrail

Do not assume that:

- previous participation automatically requires a 60-minute briefing
- executive seniority automatically requires a 60-minute briefing
- nomination means the company is ready for a deeper briefing
- a meeting with the wrong person is better than a referral

The objective is:

right contact
→ right meeting
→ right next step
