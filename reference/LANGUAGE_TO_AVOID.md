
# Language to Avoid

Avoid saying:

"You won an award."

"You've already been selected."

"This is your award."

"We chose you."

"You have to participate."

"This is guaranteed."

"You need to buy..."

"This is a sales opportunity."

"This is your last chance."

"[EXECUTIVE] is expecting my call."

unless factually true.

Also avoid traditional sales discovery language such as:

"What's your biggest pain point?"

"What keeps you up at night?"

"What's your budget?"

"What would it take to earn your business?"

The outreach should remain research-led and non-promotional.
