
# Facts Requiring Internal Confirmation

These topics should never be guessed.

Before making a specific claim, confirm the current details for the relevant Best Practices Recognition evaluation.

## Evaluation Criteria

Confirm:

- exact number of criteria,
- exact names of criteria,
- weighting,
- scoring methodology,
- category-specific criteria,
- whether criteria are universal or recognition-specific.

Do not assume that every recognition uses the same framework.

## Recognition Category

Confirm:

- exact recognition name,
- category,
- geography,
- year,
- market segment.

Do not invent a category because it sounds plausible.

## Nomination Status

Confirm:

- whether the company has been formally nominated,
- whether it is under evaluation,
- whether it has progressed to another stage,
- whether a final decision has been made.

Never imply nomination equals final recognition.

## Analyst Involvement

Confirm:

- analyst name,
- analyst role,
- whether the analyst leads the project,
- whether the analyst will attend the briefing,
- whether the analyst personally reviewed the company.

Never fabricate analyst involvement.

## Reason for Nomination

Confirm:

- specific verified achievements,
- market developments,
- research findings,
- innovation,
- growth,
- customer impact,
- differentiation.

If no specific reason is available, use general language:

"[COMPANY] surfaced during the analyst's research."

## Timeline

Confirm:

- current evaluation timeline,
- briefing deadline,
- decision timing,
- publication timing,
- recognition timing.

Do not create artificial urgency.

## Fees

Confirm:

- whether there is any fee to participate in the research,
- whether there is any fee to participate in the evaluation,
- whether later commercial licensing is optional,
- whether any exceptions apply.

## Licensing

Confirm:

- licensing structure,
- pricing,
- packages,
- duration,
- usage rights,
- logo rights,
- report rights,
- reprint rights,
- promotional rights.

Do not improvise commercial terms.

## Deliverables

Confirm exactly what the company receives.

Possible topics may include:

- award materials,
- write-up,
- report,
- certificate,
- logo,
- digital assets,
- publication,
- database listing,
- event participation.

Do not promise any item unless confirmed.

## Public Announcement

Confirm:

- whether Frost & Sullivan publishes an announcement,
- where it is published,
- whether publication occurs regardless of licensing,
- whether a database listing exists,
- whether audience or subscriber numbers may be quoted.

Do not use audience numbers unless confirmed.

## NDA and Confidentiality

Confirm:

- whether NDAs are available,
- NDA process,
- who signs,
- data handling rules,
- retention,
- confidentiality restrictions,
- whether information may be cited,
- whether information may be published.

## Editorial Review

Confirm:

- whether companies can review factual content,
- whether companies can suggest edits,
- whether companies have approval rights,
- whether Frost & Sullivan retains final editorial control.

Do not promise editorial approval unless confirmed.

## Competitor Information

Confirm what may or may not be disclosed.

Do not name other companies under evaluation unless authorized.

## Recognition Exclusivity

Confirm:

- whether only one organization is recognized,
- whether multiple companies may be recognized,
- whether the structure differs by region or category.

## Event / Gala Information

Confirm:

- whether an event exists,
- invitation rights,
- ticketing,
- attendance,
- award presentation,
- travel arrangements,
- whether participation is tied to licensing.

## Fallback Language

When information has not been confirmed, say:

"I don't want to give you the wrong information. Let me confirm the exact details for this evaluation rather than guess."

or:

"That's a good question. I want to verify the current details before I answer that."

## Information Status

Before using a company-specific or evaluation-specific fact, classify it as:

### Confirmed

The information is explicitly available for the current company or evaluation.

It may be used accurately.

### Conditional

The information may be true in some Best Practices Recognition situations but has not been confirmed for this specific evaluation.

Confirm before stating it as fact.

### Unknown

The information has not been provided.

Do not infer or invent it.

### Restricted or Sensitive

The information may exist but should only be disclosed according to the appropriate confirmed Frost & Sullivan process.

When uncertain, use:

"I don't want to give you the wrong information. Let me confirm the exact details rather than guess."
