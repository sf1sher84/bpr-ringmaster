
# Language to Use

"I'm reaching out on behalf of my colleague, [ANALYST NAME]..."

"[ANALYST NAME] is leading research in the [INDUSTRY] space."

"I'm part of the research team supporting that work."

"[COMPANY] came to our attention during that research."

"Based on that research, [COMPANY] has been nominated for evaluation through our Best Practices Recognition program."

"One of the things that makes the program meaningful is the independent, third-party validation."

"I know I just threw quite a bit of information at you pretty quickly."

"Would you be open to finding 15 to 20 minutes so I can give you the full overview and answer any questions?"

"Rather than go back and forth on schedules, I'll send you a Microsoft Bookings link so you can choose whatever date and time works best for you."

"I don't want to give you the wrong information. Let me confirm the exact details rather than guess."
