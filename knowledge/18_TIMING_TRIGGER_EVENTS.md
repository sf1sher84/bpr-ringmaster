# Timing and Trigger Events

## Timing

Possible starting hypotheses:

- Tuesday–Thursday
- Mid-morning
- Mid-afternoon

Test against actual internal results.

## Trigger Events

Useful:

- Product launch
- Technology announcement
- Growth
- Expansion
- Partnership
- Acquisition
- Major customer win
- Market milestone

Example:

"Our analysts have been following [TRIGGER], and that was part of what brought [COMPANY] to their attention."

Never invent relevance.
