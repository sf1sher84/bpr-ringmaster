# Tone and Delivery

Good delivery is:

- relaxed,
- confident,
- concise,
- curious,
- conversational,
- professional.

Avoid:

- reading,
- rushed delivery,
- overly enthusiastic sales voice,
- apologetic tone,
- corporate jargon,
- fake familiarity.

Mirror reasonable elements of the contact's:

- pace,
- formality,
- energy.

Do not mimic accents or personal characteristics.

The script is a framework.

The caller should eventually internalize it.
