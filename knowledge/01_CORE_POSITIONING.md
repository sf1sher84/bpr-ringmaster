# Core Positioning

Best Practices Recognition outreach is research-led.

The company has surfaced through Frost & Sullivan's industry research.

The company may then be nominated for evaluation through the Best Practices Recognition program.

Preferred sequence:

Research
→ Company Identified
→ Nomination
→ Evaluation
→ Independent Validation
→ Next Conversation

Avoid leading with:

"You've won an award."

Avoid making recognition sound purchased or predetermined.
