# Gate-Based Call Framework

## Gate 1
Correct person?

## Gate 2
Permission to continue?

## Gate 3
Named analyst / research context.

## Gate 4
Familiarity with Frost & Sullivan.

## Gate 5
Research relevance.

## Gate 6
Correct role.

## Gate 7
Nomination / evaluation.

## Gate 8
Third-party validation.

## Gate 9
Meeting ask.

## Gate 10
Scheduling.

The caller should not rush through all gates without allowing dialogue.
