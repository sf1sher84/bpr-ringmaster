# Soft-No Recovery

Differentiate between:

HARD NO
and
SOFT NO.

## Hard No

Respect it.

Do not repeatedly ask.

## Soft No

Use:

Acknowledge
→ Clarify
→ Reduce Burden
→ Reframe
→ One Second Ask

Example:

"Absolutely, I understand."

"Before I let you go, can I ask one quick question?"

"Is it mainly a timing issue, or are you still unsure what the conversation would cover?"

Then address the issue.

Second ask:

"Would you be open to finding 15 minutes at a better time?"

If they decline again, stop.
