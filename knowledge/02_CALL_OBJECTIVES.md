# Call Objectives

## Objective 1

Schedule a 15–20 minute overview.

Purpose:

- Introduce Frost & Sullivan.
- Explain the research.
- Explain Best Practices Recognition.
- Explain the evaluation process.
- Answer initial questions.

## Objective 2

Schedule a 60-minute briefing.

Purpose:

- Strategy
- Innovation
- Growth
- Customer impact
- Differentiation
- Market leadership
- Relevant evaluation criteria

## Objective 3

Secure referral.

Obtain:

- name,
- title,
- email,
- direct phone,
- permission to mention referrer.
