
# Role Identification

## Purpose

Use this guide to determine whether the person reached is the right contact for Frost & Sullivan Best Practices Recognition outreach.

The objective is not to force every contact into the process.

The objective is to identify the person who can best speak to the areas relevant to the research and evaluation.

---

## Core Rule

If the person is not the right contact, ask for a referral.

Do not continue explaining the entire program to someone who is not relevant to the evaluation.

Preferred language:

"I'm not sure if you're the right person for this, but does your role touch strategy, innovation, product, customer value, growth, or market positioning?"

If not:

"No problem. Who would you recommend I speak with?"

---

## Best-Fit Roles

The appropriate contact may vary by company and recognition area.

Common roles include:

- CEO
- President
- Founder
- Chief Strategy Officer
- VP Strategy
- Chief Product Officer
- VP Product
- Chief Innovation Officer
- CTO
- VP Innovation
- Chief Marketing Officer
- VP Marketing
- Corporate Communications
- Analyst Relations
- Public Relations
- Customer Experience
- Customer Success
- Business Unit Leadership
- Market Development
- Corporate Development

---

## CEO, President, or Founder

### Best For

- company strategy,
- growth,
- market leadership,
- differentiation,
- major milestones,
- overall company direction.

### Positioning

Keep the message concise.

Preferred:

"[ANALYST_NAME] is leading research in the [INDUSTRY] space, and [COMPANY] came to our attention during that work."

"The reason for the conversation is to give you the context behind the nomination and make sure our analyst team has an accurate understanding of what the company is doing in the market."

### Meeting Type

Usually:

- 15-to-20-minute overview if unfamiliar with the program,
- 60-minute analyst briefing if already familiar and highly engaged.

---

## Strategy Executive

### Best For

- strategic priorities,
- growth,
- competitive position,
- market dynamics,
- differentiation,
- expansion.

### Useful Question

"Would your role be the right place to discuss the company's strategy, market positioning, and differentiation?"

### Meeting Type

Either:

- 15-to-20-minute overview,
- 60-minute analyst briefing.

---

## Product or Innovation Leader

### Best For

- product innovation,
- technology,
- customer problems,
- differentiation,
- roadmap,
- market impact.

### Useful Question

"Would you be the right person to speak with about the innovation and product areas the analyst is evaluating?"

### Meeting Type

Often:

- 15-to-20-minute overview if unfamiliar,
- 60-minute briefing when ready for deeper discussion.

---

## Technology Leader

### Best For

- technical differentiation,
- innovation,
- platform capabilities,
- emerging technologies,
- implementation,
- market impact.

Potential titles:

- CTO
- CIO
- VP Engineering
- VP Technology
- Head of R&D

Do not assume technical leadership alone can address commercial strategy or customer value.

---

## Marketing Leader

### Best For

- market positioning,
- external messaging,
- recognition coordination,
- brand relevance,
- market differentiation.

Marketing may also help identify the appropriate executive or product participant.

---

## Communications or Public Relations

### Best For

- communications routing,
- executive access,
- external messaging,
- public announcement questions,
- internal coordination.

### Positioning

Preferred:

"We're happy to coordinate through your communications process and make sure the appropriate internal people are included."

Do not treat Communications or PR as a blocker.

---

## Analyst Relations

### Best For

- analyst coordination,
- research scope,
- methodology questions,
- briefing logistics,
- participant selection,
- confidentiality process.

### Positioning

Treat Analyst Relations as a partner.

Preferred:

"I'm happy to work through your Analyst Relations process and coordinate the appropriate participants for the discussion."

### Meeting Type

May coordinate:

- 15-to-20-minute overview,
- 60-minute analyst briefing.

---

## Customer Success or Customer Experience

### Best For

- customer impact,
- customer value,
- adoption,
- outcomes,
- retention,
- user experience.

Use when these areas are relevant to the evaluation.

---

## Business Unit Leadership

### Best For

- market strategy,
- operational performance,
- product direction,
- growth,
- customer value,
- regional or segment-specific information.

May be especially useful when the recognition applies to a specific business unit or market.

---

## Gatekeeper or Executive Assistant

The gatekeeper is usually not the evaluation contact.

The goal is to secure:

- transfer,
- referral,
- executive scheduling guidance,
- direct contact information.

Preferred:

"I'm trying to connect with the person who can speak most confidently about the company's strategy, innovation, growth, customer value, or market position."

"Who would you recommend I speak with?"

---

## Wrong Person Indicators

Possible signs:

- they say they do not handle the relevant area,
- they immediately refer the caller elsewhere,
- their role has no connection to the relevant business areas,
- they recently left the company,
- they are an external vendor,
- they do not have visibility into the company information being evaluated.

Do not push for a meeting.

Move to referral.

---

## Referral Questions

Use naturally.

"Who would you recommend I speak with?"

"Who on your team would be best suited for this?"

"Would that typically sit with Strategy, Product, Innovation, Marketing, Communications, or Analyst Relations?"

"Would you mind sharing their name and email?"

"If I reach out, is it okay if I mention that you pointed me in their direction?"

---

## Contact Validation

Where appropriate, confirm:

- current role,
- company,
- responsibility area,
- email,
- direct phone,
- relevant department.

Do not interrogate the contact.

Keep validation conversational.

---

## Multi-Person Briefings

The best 60-minute briefing may involve more than one person.

Possible combinations:

- Strategy + Product
- Product + Innovation
- Executive + Analyst Relations
- Marketing + Strategy
- Customer Success + Product
- Communications + Business Leader

The caller should not assume one person must answer every question.

---

## Role Identification Rule

A booked meeting with the wrong person is not a successful outcome.

Prioritize:

right company  
→ right person  
→ right meeting

over booking for the sake of booking.
