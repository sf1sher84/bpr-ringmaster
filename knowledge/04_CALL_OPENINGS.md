# Call Openings

## Recommended Opening

"Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Frost & Sullivan. How are you doing today?"

"I'm following up on an email I sent recently. Did you happen to see it?"

"I'm actually reaching out on behalf of my colleague, [ANALYST NAME], who's leading some research in the [INDUSTRY] space. I'm part of the research team supporting that work."

"Are you familiar with Frost & Sullivan and what we do?"

## Permission Alternative

"You weren't expecting my call. I was hoping you could help me for a brief moment."

Use when appropriate.

Do not use gimmicky or overly playful permission openers with senior executives unless the caller can deliver them naturally.
