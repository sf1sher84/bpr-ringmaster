# Objection Handling

Use:

Acknowledge
→ Clarify
→ Normalize / Reassure
→ Explain
→ Connect to Next Step

## Fee

"There's no fee to participate in the research call or evaluation."

"The evaluation process is separate from any later licensing or promotional opportunities."

## Pay-to-Play

"That's a fair question."

"This actually starts with our analysts' research. Your company came to their attention through work they were already doing in the market."

## Busy

"I completely understand. I wasn't expecting to have the full conversation right now."

"Would it be easier to find 15 minutes at a better time?"

## Send Email

"Absolutely."

"Just so I send you the most relevant information, are you already familiar with Frost & Sullivan and Best Practices Recognition, or would some background be helpful?"

## Why Us?

"[ANALYST NAME] has been researching the market, and [COMPANY] surfaced during that work."

Mention a specific reason only if verified.

## Confidentiality

"We don't expect you to share anything you're not comfortable disclosing."

"High-level ranges, percentages, or general trends may be enough."

## NDA

"We can discuss confidentiality requirements before anything sensitive is shared."

Do not promise an NDA unless confirmed.

## We Don't Need Awards

"I understand."

"This actually started with the market research rather than an awards application."

## Existing Analyst Firm

"This isn't intended to replace any existing analyst relationship."

## Deck Instead

"A deck can definitely help."

"The live conversation helps the analyst understand the story behind the information."
