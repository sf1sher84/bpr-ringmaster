# Master Call Flow

1. Confirm identity.
2. Human introduction.
3. Mention previous email.
4. Introduce named analyst.
5. Establish analyst's research project.
6. Ask familiarity with Frost & Sullivan.
7. Explain company surfaced through research.
8. Explain nomination / Best Practices Recognition.
9. Explain third-party validation.
10. Confirm correct person.
11. Ask for appropriate meeting.
12. Schedule using progressive narrowing.
13. Confirm invite.
